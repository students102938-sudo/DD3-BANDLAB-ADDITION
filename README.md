# BOSS DD-3 Studio & Vocal Rack (PWA Installable)

## Features
1. **Installable App (PWA):** Click **Install App** in the top-right header (or Add to Home Screen) to install directly on Android, iOS, Windows, or Mac.
2. **Whole-Track Exact BPM Autocorrelation:** Analyzes uploaded tracks completely before playback to lock exact BPM, plus `/2` and `x2` buttons and `1/4`, `Dotted 1/8`, `1/8`, `1/16` subdivisions.
3. **Export Processed Audio (.WAV):** Renders the entire track offline with Auto-Voice EQ, DD-3 Delay, and Studio Reverb baked in and downloads a high-quality `.wav` file.
4. **BandLab-Style Auto Voice EQ:** Automatically adapts Low Body (`180Hz`), Mud Cut (`340Hz`), Vocal Presence (`3.2kHz`), and Air (`8.5kHz`) to your voice pitch/brightness.
5. **Manual Studio Reverb:** Includes Mix, Room Size/Decay (`0.4s - 4.5s`), Tone Damping, and `22ms` vocal pre-delay.
